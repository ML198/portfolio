## Projects

### Gut Microbiome & Colorectal Cancer Risk
Analysis of metagenomic sequencing data from 245 participants to identify microbial signatures associated with colorectal cancer risk. Applied diversity metrics, differential abundance testing (MaAsLin2), and machine learning classifiers to uncover actionable biomarkers.

### Longitudinal Blood Pressure Study
Mixed-effects modeling on a 5-year cohort (n=1,200) to evaluate hypertension treatment efficacy. Survival analysis and Kaplan-Meier curves revealed a 23% reduction in major cardiovascular events among treated patients.
